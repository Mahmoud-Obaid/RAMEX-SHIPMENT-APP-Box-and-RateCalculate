Packing / Box Mode

Choose how the overall shipment dimensions used for the rate should be determined. In every mode, items are first arranged next to (and on top of) each other in the most compact shape (Option 1). Options 2 and 3 then take that arrangement and fit it into a box.

Keep Box Directions

When on, each item's Height stays vertical (upright) — items can only be turned around the vertical axis (Length and Width may swap), never tipped onto their side. Applies to the arrangement itself, and to the box in Options 2 and 3, in every mode. When off, items can be rotated freely in any direction, as before.

Arrange in Vertical Layers (closest to a cube)

When on, the app tries many layouts — one layer or several, one item or several per layer — and picks the one that comes closest to a cube / compact rectangular box while wasting as little space as possible (it scores every layout by volume x how stretched its shape is, so long thin shapes lose). Items are stacked bottom-up: the largest item forms the base, and smaller items sit on top of it, fully supported with no overhang — never a bigger item on a smaller one. This can use a bit more volume than the absolute minimum in exchange for a more cube-like, stable shape. When off, the arrangement instead minimizes total volume only (cheapest to ship), as before.

Shipment Dimensions & Weight Finder

The app works out the most compact way to place the items next to (and on top of) each other, then applies the Packing / Box Mode selected above, and returns the overall shipment dimensions and total weight for the rate calculation.

Calculate Rate

Uses the Origin and Destination addresses above, plus the overall dimensions and total weight from the Shipment Dimensions & Weight Finder, to get a shipping rate from Aramex.
